Load a DreamFactory .MOV — a cutscene, an item close-up, or a clickable object. A movie is not a video: it is a state machine of frames, each of which either takes an action or waits for a click on one of its regions. Scrub the frames, follow the machine, click the regions and see where they go, hear the audio, and edit the logic — the action codes, the targets, the region rectangles, the action-frame slots. Frame art is read-only: the frames are delta-encoded in one chain, so replacing one would smear every frame after it.

Click the picture and the movie does what it would do with that click — jump to a region's target frame, or report the exit or the chain to another file. A click outside every region does nothing, exactly as in game.
Frames are delta-encoded in one chain, so this page decodes 0…N to show frame N — going back replays the chain from the start. It is also why art is read-only: a replaced frame would leave everything after it decoding against a picture that is gone.
