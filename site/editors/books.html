Sprite Book Viewer

Source
Load a .sbk "sprite book" — Skull Cracker's cels and, for the sixteen level books, the level itself: a named plan of platforms, ladders, goals, enemy spawns and pickups, over a parallax backdrop of placed cels. Browse the cels, walk the level, see what the game put where.

This is a viewer and not an editor: it has no export. The other pages here write the format back; nothing yet writes a sprite book, because nothing reads one but this. And a level shown here is the level unrolled — the layers are drawn at the positions the book stores, and the camera that lined them up in 1996 lives in the game's own executable, not in its data.
