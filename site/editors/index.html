File Editors

Ever wondered how the game's own files are put together? Have a look for yourself! The editors also let you change files and export them again. Always dreamt of an edition in Portuguese or Chinese? Now is your chance!
Puppet Editor.PUP The layered talking-head close-ups: stance art, anchors, subtitle text. Cast Editor.CST The bodies drawn in the room — poses as a step × direction grid, walk cycles at the engine's cadence. Set Editor.SET The pre-rendered rooms you walk between: scenes, views, hotspots, roads and actor marks. Shop Editor.SHP The props drawn on top of a room — named states, their frames, and where each frame lands. Stage Editor.STG The screens that are not rooms: the UI band, the inventory, the deck plan, a mini-game board. Movie Editor.MOV Cutscenes and close-ups — not video but a state machine of frames, each acting or waiting on a click. Track Editor.TRK .SFX .11K The audio banks: play a theme and its one-shot sounds, reorder the chunks it loops through.
On every page you can look at the files that are already there. Uploading a file the DreamFactory engine can read is possible too.
