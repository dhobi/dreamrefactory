Maze Viewer

Source
Load one of Lunicus's mazes — the station's two floors, the city and its building, the engine rooms, the hive and the final chamber. A maze is a grid of cells you stand in facing one of four ways, joined by short pre-rendered films, one for every step and every quarter turn. See the grid, stand anywhere in it, and walk it the way the game does.

This is a viewer and not an editor: it has no export. The maze holds no palette of its own; the game draws it in a palette kept in LUNIRES.DLL, which this page fetches with the maze, and which you can drop here with an uploaded one.
