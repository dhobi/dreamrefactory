Source
Load one of RedJack's rooms, a .sett. A room is nodes you stand at and look round a sphere from, roads that walk the camera from one node to the next on film, scenes of a few fixed views, quads a click can find, and stars the props and actors are put on. Stand anywhere, look round, walk the roads, and see the whole room from above.

The quads and the stars are editable: their names, where they are and, for a quad, its size and how it is turned. The export writes each edit over the bytes it came from and leaves every other byte as it was. The spheres, the films and the scripts are read-only.

Quads
What a click can find: a w × h rectangle set down at a point, turned and pitched. Degrees here; the file keeps radians. A renamed quad is one the room's scripts no longer find by its old name.
Stars
The named points propstar and walkonpath reach. A star with a route to another is marked; moving it leaves the route's points where they were.
