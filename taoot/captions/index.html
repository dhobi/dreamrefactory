Caption editor

Titanic speaks some lines it never prints: the radio news and the landlady at the London flat, the Sasha and Zeitel gossip on the boat deck, the voices behind cabin doors, the fencing master, the narration over the opening credits. The port can caption them for players who cannot hear them, but only from a transcript. Here you can listen to each clip in your language and write down what is really said.

Not original data. These captions do not come from the game's files. They are a best-effort transcript: a speech recogniser's first draft, corrected by hand. Please write what you hear, not a better line, and who says it. Tick guess where you are unsure and listened once you have checked a line by ear.
Edition:

Your edits stay in this browser until you send or save them. Send to GitHub opens a new issue with the lines you changed, for you to submit (a GitHub account is needed). Or save the file and put it in taoot/src/captions/ in a pull request. Keys: Enter next clip and play it, Shift+Enter previous, Ctrl+Enter play again.
