A Tale of the Wired West RE

RE is for reverse-engineered. This is not an emulation: it is a version of CyberFlix's DreamFactory engine implemented in JavaScript (TypeScript), playing their 1995 Western. It plays through to the credits, but it is younger than the original engine by some years.
Play around, try the editors and report bugs straight to us on GitHub.
